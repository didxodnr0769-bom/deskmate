Image tool edit of the existing packaged chelsea-pet.png, 2026-10-23.
No private photographs or motion-capture data were used. PNG metadata retained.

Create animation RIG PARTS, not a walk sprite sequence. Preserve Chelsea's gray
longhair tabby identity, white muzzle/chest/paws, yellow-green eyes and existing
soft painterly 2D art. Transparent PNG with exactly two columns and four rows,
eight isolated connected parts, generous gutters and no labels, background or shadow.
Row 1: matching right and left profile torso + neck + head + fluffy tail, all legs
removed under the body, natural belly fur. These are disassembled animation assets,
not injured animals; no wounds. Keep the original cat's body proportions and coat.
Row 2: upper foreleg (shoulder/elbow) and lower foreleg (elbow/wrist), straight
nearly vertical fur textures with rounded overlapping ends, no paw/body.
Row 3: upper hind thigh and lower hind shank, thick/slim gray striped fur segments
with rounded overlapping ends, no paw/body.
Row 4: separate white front and hind paws in right-facing profile, flat undersides
and short fur at the top to overlap the leg.

The actual gait phases are authored in chelsea-gait.ts and rendered with affine
part transforms. The tool was not asked to invent successive walking poses.
The resulting parts do not themselves establish natural motion. See
docs/chelsea-gait.md for the rejected atlas audit, sources and visual-review limits.
